eNuminous Laws, Axioms, Hypotheses & Theorems

A browsable catalog of named ideas and Lean theorem declarations found in Matthew Wright’s public repositories. Search across categories, descriptions, repositories, and source files.

How to read this catalog It records what the repositories state; inclusion is not evidence that a claim is true. The source audit reports 813 Lean theorem/lemma declarations (586 distinct names) across 20 repositories, but says those projects were not rebuilt for the catalog. A Lean proof checks a formal mathematical statement under its definitions and assumptions; it does not by itself validate a physical interpretation or industry application. Famous open-problem claims shown here are not accepted by mathematicians.

What the labels mean

Axioms

Starting assumptions or principles adopted inside a framework. The catalog reports no Lean axiom declarations in the searched files.

Laws and hypotheses

Named relations and testable or interpretive proposals. Their evidence varies; “law” is a source label, not a finding of independent validation.

Theorems

Formal results, written proofs, unproved statements, and reported open-problem claims are separated by the source catalog. The search can filter each group.
